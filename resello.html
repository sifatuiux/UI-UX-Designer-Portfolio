Resello – Dashboard

Product Overview

  • Resello is a reseller management dashboard designed to give online resellers a clear, single view of their sales, orders, and earnings.
  • The project explores how a dense, data-heavy tool can still feel calm and approachable for someone running a business on their own.
  • The experience brings together performance metrics, sales and order trends, and a live orders table into one screen a reseller can check in seconds.
  • The design balances the clarity a business dashboard needs with a warm, personal tone that doesn't feel like enterprise software.
Role UI/UX Designer
Client Resello
Duration 2 Weeks
Live Site Resello.design

Problem Statement

Resellers often juggle orders across chats, spreadsheets, and marketplaces, with no single place to see how their business is actually doing.

The challenge was to create an experience that could:

  • Consolidate sales, orders, and earnings into one clear view
  • Make dense business data easy to scan in seconds, not minutes
  • Help resellers instantly spot what needs their attention today
  • Build confidence around earnings and payout tracking
  • Keep the interface simple despite the amount of data on screen

Core Problem

How might we help a reseller understand their business performance at a glance, and know exactly what needs their attention today?

Possible Solution

A clean, data-forward dashboard that combines key business metrics, visual trends, and actionable order tracking in a single glance.

Key solution areas included:

  • Quick-glance stat cards for total sales, orders, earnings, and pending orders
  • Sales and orders overview charts to spot trends without digging through data
  • A detailed latest orders table with color-coded status tags for fast scanning
  • A focused sidebar grouping core reseller tasks — products, orders, earnings, profile
  • A friendly, personalized greeting that keeps the tool feeling approachable, not corporate

User Research

The research focused on what independent resellers actually need to see the moment they log in.

  • What numbers does a reseller check first thing in the day?
  • How do resellers currently track orders and payouts?
  • What makes a business dashboard feel overwhelming versus useful?
  • What information actually needs immediate action versus just context?

Key Findings

At-a-Glance Metrics Save Time

Resellers want total sales, orders, earnings, and pending items visible the moment the dashboard loads, without clicking in.

Trends Matter More Than Totals

A single total feels flat — seeing sales and orders as a trend over months helps resellers judge whether the business is actually growing.

Status Visibility Reduces Follow-up

Clear, color-coded order status cuts down on resellers manually chasing customers or suppliers to check where an order stands.

Payouts Need Constant Reassurance

Since earnings directly affect income, resellers want withdrawable versus pending amounts clearly separated at all times.

User Persona

The user persona represents a typical Resello user: an independent reseller who needs to track her business performance quickly between other daily responsibilities.

Goals

  • Quickly check how much she's earned and what's still pending.
  • Spot sales trends without digging through spreadsheets.
  • Know immediately which orders need her attention today.
  • Track payouts without repeatedly messaging support.

Pain Points

  • Juggling orders across chats and marketplaces makes tracking difficult.
  • Hard to tell at a glance which orders are stuck or delayed.
  • Manual spreadsheet tracking is slow and error-prone.
  • Payout timelines feel unclear without a dedicated view.

Typography

The typography system pairs a clean, geometric sans-serif for headings with a highly legible body type built for numbers — the heading type keeps the dashboard feeling modern and confident, while the body type keeps stats, tables, and labels easy to scan at speed.

Used Colors

The Resello color system uses a confident purple as the primary brand and action color, paired with a soft neutral grey background that keeps dense data comfortable to read for long dashboard sessions.

What This Project Taught Me

01

Data Needs Hierarchy, Not Just Charts

Adding more charts didn't make the dashboard clearer — ordering information by what actually needs action first did.

02

Status Color Coding Speeds Decisions

Color-coded order statuses let resellers scan an entire table in seconds instead of reading every row individually.

03

Personal Touches Make Data Feel Less Cold

A simple personalized greeting made a numbers-heavy dashboard feel like a tool built for one person, not generic enterprise software.
