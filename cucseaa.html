CSE Connect – CUCSEAA

Product Overview

  • CSE Connect is a student and alumni management platform designed for the Computer Science & Engineering department of City University, Bangladesh, and its alumni association, CUCSEAA.
  • Students and alumni are both primary users. The platform brings jobs, events, mentorship, memories, and community requests into one verified space instead of scattered social media groups.
  • Every profile, post, and event is reviewed by an admin, so members can trust who they are talking to and what they are reading.
  • The design balances a warm community feel, professional networking, and simple administration across a responsive website.
Role UI/UX Designer
Client CUCSEAA, City University
Duration 5 Weeks
Live Site CSEConnect.Design

Problem Statement

CSE graduates and current students at City University mostly connect through Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, and personal contacts. Information gets buried, nobody can confirm who is genuine, and many alumni slowly lose touch with the department.

The challenge was to create a platform that could:

  • Give students and alumni equal access to one shared community
  • Verify every member before granting access
  • Make jobs, events, and announcements easy to find and act on
  • Help students reach senior alumni for guidance and referrals
  • Preserve the department's memories and history in one place
  • Reduce the manual workload of the alumni association and admin team

Core Problem

How might we build a trusted, admin-managed platform where students and alumni can connect for careers, events, and community support without losing the warmth of a department family?

Possible Solution

The proposed solution was a centralized community website that treats students and alumni as equal members while keeping every action verified and organized.

Key solution areas included:

  • A welcoming home page with live community stats and clear entry points
  • Verified alumni directory with search by name, batch, or ID
  • Event listings with quick registration and clear dates, venues, and attendance
  • A community feed for jobs, job-wanted posts, announcements, and urgent blood requests
  • A memories gallery to celebrate reunions, seminars, and campus life
  • Role-based profiles with separate student and alumni information

This approach lets members feel part of the community first, then gives them the tools to find opportunities, people, and support when they need it.

User Research

The research focused on understanding what students and alumni expect from a department community platform.

  • How do students and alumni stay in touch today?
  • What makes members trust a post, a job, or a profile?
  • How do students find seniors for career advice?
  • What stops alumni from staying active after graduation?
  • What kind of urgent help does the community need most?

Key Findings

Verified Trust

Members are more willing to engage when every profile and post is confirmed by an admin as a genuine City University CSE student or alumnus.

Career Access

Students want job openings and referrals from people in their own network, and alumni are happy to post them when it takes little effort.

Easy Events

Events announced through posters and group chats are easy to miss. Clear dates, venues, and one-tap registration increase participation.

Findable People

Searching by name, batch, or student ID is how members actually look for classmates and seniors, so the directory has to support it.

Shared Memories

Reunion and campus photos bring alumni back to the platform and give current students a sense of the department's history.

Help When Needed

The community already uses group chats for urgent needs like blood requests. A structured format makes these posts faster to read and act on.

User Persona

The persona represents a typical student member of CSE Connect who wants early career guidance from alumni. It helps define the motivations, expectations, and pain points that shaped the platform, alongside the alumni needs identified in research.

Goals

  • Get honest career advice from seniors and alumni.
  • Find genuine internships and job openings early.
  • Never miss department events, workshops, and seminars.
  • Build a professional network before graduating.

Pain Points

  • Information is spread across many Facebook and WhatsApp groups.
  • Hard to tell whether a job post or profile is genuine.
  • Difficult to find and approach alumni working in her field.
  • Event details are often shared late or get buried in chats.

Typography

The typography system was designed to feel friendly, professional, and easy to scan across dense pages like directories and feeds. A clean modern sans-serif is used throughout, with clear weight and size differences for titles, card details, and body text. Section titles highlight one accent word in red to reinforce the brand.

Used Colors

The CSE Connect color system pairs a confident red with soft grays and a deep charcoal. Red matches the CUCSEAA logo and marks actions, active states, and key highlights. Light grays keep cards and forms clean, while charcoal anchors the footer and the jobs section with strong contrast.

Mid-Fidelity Wireframe

The wireframing stage defined the structure of the home, events, alumni, memories, posts, and profile pages. It set the shared header and footer, card layouts, filters, and the differences between student and alumni profiles before any visual styling was applied.

User Interface

The final interface brings together a consistent card system, real community photography, and a red-accented identity to make the platform feel official yet welcoming.

01 -

A Front Door for the Whole Department

The home page opens with the CUCSEAA logo and live community numbers, then walks through six feature cards, a dark jobs section, and alumni success stories. New visitors understand what the platform offers before they scroll far.

02 -

Events Without the Poster Hunt

One large card shows the very next event, followed by a grid of upcoming ones. Each card carries a red date badge, venue, time, and registration count, so members can decide in a glance and register right away.

03 -

Finding People, Not Scrolling Lists

The executive committee carousel puts faces to the association, while the directory lets members search by name, batch, or ID. Cards show graduation year, role, company, and location, with social links ready for outreach.

04 -

Twenty Years in One Gallery

A masonry grid of reunions, sports days, and convocations turns years of events into a single scroll. Hovering a photo reveals its event name, date, and place, and members can add their own through Share a Memory.

05 -

One Feed, Many Kinds of Need

Filters for Hiring, Job Wanted, Need Blood, Announcement, and General keep the feed easy to sort. Role and category tags show who is posting and why, and urgent blood requests use a structured layout so the key details stand out at once.

06 -

Profiles That Fit the Person

Students and alumni share the same settings layout, but alumni get an extra Professional Info tab for company, role, and location. A summary card at the top shows the details other members will see, so nothing surprises the user.

07 -

Trust in Plain Language

The privacy and terms pages use a contents sidebar and numbered sections so members can jump to what they need. Version and update date are shown up front, and the copy explains data use without legal jargon.

What This Project Taught Me

01

Two Users, One Experience

Students and alumni needed the same layout, with differences shown through tags and profile fields rather than separate designs.

02

Verification Builds Community

Admin approval and visible role tags gave members the confidence to post, apply, and reach out to people they had never met.

03

Design for Urgent Moments

Designing the blood request post taught me to put the most critical details first, so people can act in seconds.
