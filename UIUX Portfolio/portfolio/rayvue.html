RayVue – Trading

Product Overview

  • RayVue Trading is a trading journal platform designed to help traders track, analyze, and learn from their trades in one place.
  • Instead of relying on spreadsheets or memory, traders can review their performance, behavior, strategies, and trading history through a structured dashboard.
  • The landing page was designed to communicate this value clearly while making a complex financial product feel professional, focused, and approachable.
Role UI/UX Designer
Client Personal Project
Duration 1 Week
Live Site Rayvue.Design

Problem Statement

Traders often have valuable information scattered across spreadsheets, broker platforms, notes, and memory.

The challenge was to create a clear digital experience that could:

  • Bring trade information into one place
  • Make performance easier to understand
  • Help traders recognize behavioral patterns
  • Present complex analytics without overwhelming users

Core Problem

How might we present a powerful trading journal in a way that feels clear, trustworthy, and easy to understand?

Possible Solution

I designed RayVue as a focused trading journal that brings tracking, analytics, behavioral review, and reporting into one platform.

The landing page communicates the product through:

  • Clear, performance-focused messaging
  • Dashboard previews that show the product in context
  • Structured feature sections
  • A simple three-step workflow
  • Social proof and transparent pricing

This approach lets traders quickly understand what RayVue does, see it in action, and move confidently toward starting a free trial.

User Research

The research focused on understanding what traders need when reviewing their performance and where existing workflows create friction.

  • How do traders currently record their trades?
  • What information matters most during review?
  • How can analytics become easier to understand?
  • What makes a trading platform feel trustworthy?

Key Findings

Information Gets Fragmented

Traders may use different tools for trade records, analysis, notes, and performance tracking.

Numbers Need Context

Metrics become more useful when traders can connect them with strategies, sessions, and behavior.

Clarity Builds Trust

Financial products need clear information and a professional visual language to feel credible.

Progress Should Feel Visible

Showing performance over time helps traders recognize patterns and make more informed decisions.

Competitive Analysis

Most competitors focus heavily on features and analytics. RayVue's direction was to make those capabilities feel clear, structured, and easy to understand, especially for traders who may find large amounts of trading data overwhelming.

Typography

The typography balances financial professionalism with modern readability. Inter builds strong visual hierarchy for headings, while the same family keeps longer descriptions, metrics, and supporting details easy to scan at a glance.

Used Colors

The visual system uses a professional, restrained palette suited to a financial product. The primary blue builds brand recognition and highlights key actions, while darker neutral tones provide structure, depth, and contrast across the interface.

Mid-Fidelity Wireframe

The wireframing stage established the single-page structure, content hierarchy, and rhythm before applying the final visual style. It helped organize the flow from hero, features, and workflow through pricing and social proof into one uninterrupted journey.

User Interface

The final interface brings the experience together through soft visual hierarchy, spacious layouts, and clean Inter typography throughout. The design intentionally avoids visual noise and distracting patterns, allowing each section to feel like a natural, focused step within the journey.

What This Project Taught Me

01

Show, Don't Just Tell

For SaaS products, showing the actual interface can communicate value much faster than describing every feature.

02

Data Needs Hierarchy

When a product contains many numbers and metrics, visual hierarchy becomes essential for keeping the experience understandable.

03

Trust Is Part of UX

For a financial product, credibility isn't only about the features. The structure, clarity, and visual language all contribute to trust.
