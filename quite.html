Quite – Peace

Product Overview

  • Quite is a non-profit mindfulness website created to offer users a moment of calm.
  • The project has no business goal, no data capture, and no conversion intent. Its purpose is to create a simple, quiet digital space where users can pause, breathe, and reconnect with themselves.
  • The experience is designed as a single-page journey, encouraging slow, uninterrupted scrolling rather than task-driven interaction.
Role UI/UX Designer
Client Personal Project
Duration 1 Week
Live Site Quite.Design

Problem Statement

Digital experiences constantly ask users to interact, decide, and move quickly, leaving little space for simply being present.

The challenge was to create an experience that could:

  • Offer a quiet moment without demanding attention
  • Help users reconnect with their thoughts and body
  • Introduce mindfulness without making it feel complicated
  • Encourage slow, uninterrupted exploration

Core Problem

How might we create a digital experience that helps users pause and reconnect without adding another task to their day?

Possible Solution

A minimal, single-page mindfulness experience built around calm visual hierarchy and gentle practices.

Key solution areas included:

  • A quiet opening that immediately reduces visual noise
  • Breath and awareness sections for guided attention
  • Simple grounding practices that require no preparation
  • A slow scrolling journey instead of task-heavy navigation
  • A gentle closing invitation to pause and begin again

This approach lets users arrive without pressure, move through the page at their own pace, and leave feeling a little more grounded than when they came in.

User Research

Focused on understanding how a digital experience can support short moments of mindfulness without becoming another demanding task.

  • What makes a digital space feel calm rather than distracting?
  • How can mindfulness practices feel simple enough to begin immediately?
  • What helps users stay present instead of rushing through content?
  • How much guidance is helpful before the experience feels overwhelming?

Key Findings

Less Demands More

A calm experience should not constantly ask users to click, choose, or complete something.

Simplicity Creates Space

Minimal content and clear hierarchy allow users to focus on the moment rather than the interface.

Gentle Guidance Helps

Short practices give users direction without turning mindfulness into a rigid process, and help them relax.

Slow Interaction Matters

The experience should encourage users to pause and move through content at their own pace.

User Persona

Represents a typical user looking for a short moment of calm during an otherwise busy day. This persona helps define the user's motivations, expectations, goals, and pain points throughout the experience.

Goals

  • Take a short mental pause.
  • Reduce everyday tension.
  • Reconnect with the present moment.
  • Practice mindfulness without complexity.

Pain Points

  • Digital spaces often feel overwhelming.
  • Meditation platforms can feel too structured.
  • Limited time makes long practices difficult.
  • Too many interactions can become distracting.

Typography

The typography system pairs Playfair Display for section titles with Inter for body content — the serif creates a reflective and gentle character, while Inter keeps the supporting content clean, readable, and unobtrusive.

Used Colors

The Quite color system uses a soft, restrained palette designed to reduce visual stimulation and support the feeling of calm throughout the experience.

Blue

Grey

Mid-Fidelity Wireframe

The wireframing stage established the single-page structure, content hierarchy, and rhythm before applying the final visual style. It helped organize awareness, breath, grounding, practices, and reflections into one uninterrupted journey.

User Interface

The final interface brings the experience together through soft visual hierarchy, spacious layouts, Playfair Display headings, and clean Inter body text. The design intentionally avoids visual noise and conversion-focused patterns, allowing each section to feel like a natural pause within the journey.
